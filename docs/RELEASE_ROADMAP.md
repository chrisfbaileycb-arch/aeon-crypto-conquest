# Release roadmap

## Phase 1 — prototype (included)

Playable loop, local persistence, free/VIP product surfaces, simulated rewarded placement, disclosures, audio, tests, and Capacitor configuration.

## Phase 2 — production services

1. Replace VIP preview with Google Play Billing and server-side purchase verification.
2. Replace the simulated reward with AdMob rewarded ads. Only grant rewards after the verified completion callback.
3. Add a remote, curated campaign feed for house apps and disclosed affiliate offers.
4. Add account/cloud-save only if cross-device progress is required; anonymous local play is the privacy-light default.
5. Add analytics with consent, data minimization, deletion, and a published privacy policy.

## Phase 3 — safety and store readiness

- Commission a current Google Play policy review, complete the Data safety form, content rating, ads declaration, and target-audience settings.
- Add age gating/parental controls appropriate to the final rating and markets.
- Add configurable session reminders, spend controls, cooldowns, and self-exclusion from promotional notifications.
- Independently simulate the economy and publish accurate odds/RTP information if required by the final market classification.
- Test billing restoration, offline behavior, ad failure paths, accessibility, low-end Android devices, and account deletion.

## Explicit non-goals

No real-money gambling, real or tokenized crypto, withdrawals, transferable value, financial advice, investment simulation presented as expected return, or paid random items with real-world value.
